# Bloody Mary

**Age:** Unknown  
**Size:** 1.8
**Magic:** blood
**Source of Magic:** the demon lord
---

## Lore
Mary was born in the small village of Akamori, a remote settlement that had endured constant demon attacks for generations. Her father was the village's greatest swordsman, dedicating his life to protecting its people from the creatures that roamed the surrounding lands.

Inspired by him, Mary began training with a katana at a young age. Year after year, she honed her skills with relentless discipline until she eventually surpassed even her father, becoming the finest swordswoman the village had ever known. When her father died, Mary inherited his katana and began wielding both her own blade and his, carrying on his legacy as the village's protector.

As the years passed, the demons realized they could no longer defeat Mary in direct combat. Instead, they changed their strategy. They burned crops, poisoned wells, slaughtered livestock, ambushed supply caravans, and sabotaged anything that kept the village alive, hoping to starve its people and force Mary into a losing battle.

Stories of the lone swordswoman defending an isolated village eventually reached the kingdom. Impressed by her reputation, the royal demon-hunter army sent a commander to recruit her. Mary initially refused, unwilling to abandon the home her father had died protecting. The commander, however, promised that both she and every villager would be welcomed and protected within the kingdom's walls. Accepting that her people would have a safer future there, Mary agreed.

Over the following years, Mary quickly distinguished herself within the royal demon-hunter army. Her lifetime of experience fighting demons, combined with the leadership skills she had inherited while protecting her village, allowed her to rise through the ranks until she became one of the kingdom's youngest and most respected generals.

Even after joining the kingdom, Mary regularly returned to Akamori to ensure that her former home remained safe.

During one of these visits, she found the village completely destroyed. In the center stood a lone demon feasting on what appeared to be human corpses. Confused, Mary knew something was wrong. No travelers had recently passed through the area, and no demon had ever managed to massacre an entire visiting party without word reaching the kingdom. Despite her suspicions, she ordered her squad to charge alongside her.

As they advanced, the soldiers suddenly began losing control of their bodies, turning their weapons against one another in a frenzy of bloodshed. When Mary closed the distance, the corpses dissolved into pools of blood, revealing that the entire scene had been an illusion designed to lure her into a trap.

The demon revealed himself to be a Demon Lord, one whose armies had spent over two centuries unsuccessfully trying to conquer the region. Unable to remain in the human world for long because of the constant struggle for territory and survival among the Demon Lords and Demon Kings in Hell, he had come personally to create the perfect puppet. By possessing the kingdom's greatest demon-hunter general, he could continue his conquest from afar.

Using his overwhelming power, the Demon Lord immobilized Mary and forced his own blood into her body, intending to dominate her mind, body, and soul.

However, Mary carried powerful protection seals issued by the royal demon-hunter army. The seals were designed to stop demonic corruption, but they had never encountered magic as ancient and powerful as that of a Demon Lord. Instead of expelling the blood, the seals trapped it within Mary's body.

Normally, such a fusion would have been impossible. But Mary had been born completely magicless. With no magical essence of her own for the demonic power to overwhelm or replace, the trapped blood merged directly with her own.

The battle that followed took place within Mary's very being. She endured unbearable pain as the Demon Lord attempted to consume her body, mind, and soul. She felt as though her body would tear itself apart while an endless darkness threatened to swallow her completely. Refusing to surrender, Mary's will proved stronger. She suppressed the Demon Lord's consciousness, preventing him from taking control, before using her newly awakened blood powers to destroy his physical body.

Although victorious, Mary soon realized the truth.

The demons she and her father had fought throughout their lives had been among the weakest of demonkind. Against a true Demon Lord, she had survived only through an impossible chain of circumstances.

She also realized that she was no longer fully human.

The Demon Lord's blood had permanently fused with her own, granting her terrifying new abilities while forever changing her body. Unsure of exactly what she had become, Mary returned to the kingdom carrying a burden no one else could truly understand.

What she did not know was that the Demon Lord had not truly died.

His consciousness had merely fallen dormant within her blood, patiently waiting for the day it might awaken once more.

After discovering that she had become something neither fully human nor fully demon, Mary realized she could never return to the kingdom she had once sworn to protect. Fearing that those she cared about would see her as a monster, she chose exile instead and began wandering the world in search of a place where she might belong.

Throughout her travels, Mary found herself constantly surrounded by conflict. Many humans feared her unnatural powers and viewed her as a threat, while monsters sought to destroy her because she was neither truly human nor one of their own. Every battle left the ground drenched in blood. Before long, humans began calling her Bloody Mary, while monsters mocked her as the Fake Demon.

Years passed, and although Mary survived every battle, she could feel a growing darkness within herself. Each time she relied on her blood powers, the influence of the dormant Demon Lord became stronger. Its whispers grew louder, tempting her with promises of greater strength and urging her to embrace the power she had tried so desperately to resist.

Everything changed when Mary crossed paths with Queen Cruel.

Recognizing the queen's royal status, Mary warned her to stay away, believing herself to have become a hideous demon who would only bring harm to those around her. Queen Cruel ignored the warning. Rather than seeing a monster, she saw only a confused young woman who had lost her place in the world.

Mary, whose entire life had been built upon discipline, strength, and the respect she had earned as one of the kingdom's greatest generals, took the queen's words as an insult. Drawing both katanas, she launched a relentless assault.

The battle quickly became one-sided.

Queen Cruel effortlessly blocked every strike, revealing a level of strength that Mary had never encountered before. As the fight dragged on, Mary realized she could not win through skill alone. At her weakest moment, the dormant Demon Lord began whispering within her mind, promising enough power to defeat Queen Cruel if she would simply surrender to him.

Driven into desperation, Mary stabbed herself with one of her katanas, unleashing the full power of the Demon Lord's blood that flowed through her body. The promise of greater strength proved true, but it came at a terrible cost. The act awakened the Demon Lord's dormant consciousness, allowing him to seize control of Mary's body.

Unable to resist, Mary lost consciousness.

When she awoke, Mary found herself restrained within a healing chamber. Although her body had survived, the enchanted chains that bound her proved impossible to break.

Queen Cruel revealed that, while Mary had been unconscious, the Demon Lord had desperately revealed everything in the hope of earning mercy and securing his own freedom. His pleas had fallen on deaf ears. Having spent her own life with a far more powerful Demon King sealed within her, Queen Cruel neither feared nor pitied the creature living inside Mary.

Seeing that Mary had survived the struggle, Queen Cruel presented her with a simple choice.

She could die and finally be freed from the burden of the Demon Lord and the life she had come to hate.

Or she could stand beside Queen Cruel, abandon her fear of what she had become, and once again become the great warrior she had been.

Mary accepted Queen Cruel's offer and chose to live.

Recognizing the reputation Mary had earned during her years of wandering, Queen Cruel embraced the name that humanity had given her—Bloody Mary. From that day forward, Mary abandoned her former title and became known by the name that had inspired both fear and legend.

Under Queen Cruel's guidance, Bloody Mary spent years learning to control the Demon Lord sealed within her blood. Through relentless training, discipline, and unwavering determination, she gradually mastered her powers instead of allowing them to master her. In time, she became one of Queen Cruel's greatest generals and one of the kingdom's most trusted warriors.

Queen Cruel foresaw that Bloody Mary's half-demonic nature would grant her a far longer lifespan than an ordinary human. Knowing that Mary would outlive her, the queen spoke to her shortly before her death, freeing her from any obligation to remain in the kingdom after she was gone.

When Queen Cruel passed away, Bloody Mary honored her final wish and departed.

Rather than wandering without purpose as she once had, Bloody Mary set out to find others who, like herself, no longer belonged among ordinary humans. During her journey, she forged a pact with Hell, the ruler of the Infernal Realm. In exchange for her service, Hell granted Bloody Mary and those she rescued a place to live within his domain.

Over the years, Bloody Mary searched tirelessly for humans who had been transformed, cursed, or otherwise rejected because of what they had become. She led them to Hell, where they could begin new lives free from persecution. As their numbers grew, so too did Bloody Mary's own army, composed of people who had once been outcasts but had finally found a place they could call home.

When the Devil overthrew Hell and seized control of the Infernal Realm, Bloody Mary chose to support the new ruler during the invasion of Heaven. Her loyalty was not born from a desire for conquest, but from a determination to protect the sanctuary she had built for her people. By ensuring the Devil's victory, she secured their right to remain in Hell.

Years later, when Queen Cruel returned to life, Bloody Mary immediately resumed her service. Though she had built her own following and earned her own legend, her loyalty to the queen who had saved her from despair had never faded.

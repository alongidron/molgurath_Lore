# Father Crow

**Father Crow** is one of the most powerful Overlords, feared for his extraordinary combat skills, ruthless nature, and cruelty. As a merciless conqueror, he commands one of the largest armies among the Overlords, numbering approximately four million soldiers.

His god-blessed weapon, the Halberd of the Abyss, allows him to unleash devastating green waves of Abyss magic capable of cutting through anything in their path. However, unleashing this attack requires Father Crow to pull his halberd all the way back and perform a full-power swing, requiring approximately ten seconds of preparation. Once released, the wave travels forward, expanding as it moves. Due to the lengthy preparation, Father Crow primarily uses this ability to counter large-scale magical attacks

Father Crow marks his soldiers and followers with magical crow masks that enhance their abilities with Abyss magic. However, these masks also serve as a means of control, killing their wearers if removed. Through the masks, Father Crow can witness battles and learn from the combat experiences of those wearing them, granting him extraordinary combat awareness and earning him the reputation of the **Overlord with the fastest reaction speed**.

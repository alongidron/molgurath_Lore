# Father Crow

Father Crow is one of the most powerful Overlords, feared for his extraordinary combat skills, ruthless nature, and cruelty. As a merciless conqueror, he commands one of the largest armies among the Overlords, numbering approximately four million soldiers.

Through his Abyss magic, Father Crow can take on three different forms, each with distinct advantages. His child form provides his greatest movement speed at the cost of physical strength. His adult form offers a balance of physical power, speed, and magic, while his old form, his true form, sacrifices mobility in exchange for immense magical power.

Father Crow strives to master every type of weapon, allowing him to understand their mechanics, strengths, and weaknesses. He favors using a rapier in his child form to eliminate weaker opponents as quickly as possible, taking advantage of his exceptional movement speed. However, when facing powerful opponents, he relies on his ultimate weapon, the Halberd of the Abyss.

His god-blessed halberd can be infused with Abyss magic to unleash a devastating green wave capable of cutting through anything in its path. The wave expands as it travels, but requires lengthy preparation and a full-power swing to unleash, making it difficult to use against fast or aggressive opponents.

Father Crow marks his soldiers and followers with magical crow masks that enhance their abilities with Abyss magic. However, these masks also serve as a means of control, killing their wearers if removed. Through the masks, Father Crow can witness battles and learn from the combat experiences of those wearing them, granting him extraordinary combat awareness and earning him the reputation of the Overlord with the fastest reaction speed.

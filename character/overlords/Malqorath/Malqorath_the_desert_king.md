# Malqorath

**Age:** Unknown  
**Size:** 2.20 m  
**Magic:** Sand, Divine Magic (Egyptian Gods)  
**Source of Magic:** Natural sand magic and the divine chain created by Ra, granting Malqorath access to the combined magic and mana of the Egyptian gods.
---

## Lore


Malqorath was born in a small mummy village within the desert kingdom of Khemet-Ra, a land shaped by endless dunes and ancient burial magic. His people lived quietly among the sands, preserving forgotten rituals and guarding the remnants of a long-dead civilization.
That peace ended when a sand dragon, corrupted by chaos, descended upon the village. Twisted by unstable power, the creature brought devastation, reducing the settlement to ruin. Despite its overwhelming strength, the corruption consumed the dragon from within, and it eventually collapsed after its rampage.
Malqorath and a small group of survivors were the only ones left alive.
Standing before the fallen corpse of the dragon, they did not mourn for long. Instead, they adapted. The survivors harvested the creature’s remains, forging weapons and armor from its bones, scales, and corrupted essence. What once destroyed their home became the source of their strength.
With nothing left to return to, Malqorath and the others abandoned the ruins of their village and set out into the desert, carrying the legacy of the dragon with them.
For years, Malqorath and the surviving warriors wandered the desert, offering aid to those in need. Their strength and discipline earned them respect across scattered settlements, and over time, their group grew into a powerful force. What began as a band of survivors slowly transformed into a rising empire. Eventually, they established a new kingdom, with Malqorath as its leader.
Years later, the sun god Ra descended upon the kingdom.
Malqorath did not understand why a god would seek him. Ra revealed his purpose—to choose a champion worthy of ruling the desert. Malqorath initially refused, unwilling to bind himself to divine will. However, Ra insisted that among all rulers, Malqorath alone possessed the balance of strength and mercy required to unite the desert.
In time, Malqorath accepted.
Upon his acceptance, Ra forged a divine chain, channeling the power of the desert gods into both Malqorath and his weapon. The moment he grasped it, he felt overwhelming power flow through him. Testing it, he struck a decorative pot, reducing it to dust instantly. What shocked him most was not the destruction—but the absence of strain. His strongest techniques no longer drained him.
Ra explained that Malqorath now carried the power and mana of the desert gods themselves.
With this blessing came a command: unify the desert.
Malqorath began a campaign across the sands, traveling from kingdom to kingdom. Though he embraced battle and grew to enjoy the challenge, he ruled with restraint. Kings who surrendered were spared, and bloodshed was kept to a minimum. Only those who refused unity entirely were destroyed.
One by one, the desert kingdoms fell under his rule.
In time, all were united.
Malqorath rose as the sole ruler of the sands—the Desert King, chosen by the gods, wielder of their combined power, and the unifier of all desert realms.
For years after his ascension, Malqorath ruled as Desert King, defending his unified kingdom from external threats. Warlords, invaders, and even lesser overlords attempted to conquer the desert, drawn by its power and divine blessing. All were defeated. Under Malqorath’s rule, the desert became nearly untouchable.
That changed when Velmora arrived.
Unlike others, she did not come with an army. She came alone.
Malqorath immediately recognized her as a threat and prepared for battle. What followed was one of the few fights in which Malqorath was pushed beyond control. His divine power proved ineffective—Velmora avoided his attacks effortlessly, using teleportation and advanced magic to stay out of reach.
Realizing direct combat would fail, Malqorath unleashed one of his most powerful techniques, reducing Velmora’s body to sand.
The victory was an illusion.
She reformed instantly behind him and struck with overwhelming force, sending him across the battlefield. For the first time since becoming Desert King, Malqorath was on the verge of defeat.
Before the battle could end, Ra intervened.
Understanding the situation, Velmora chose not to continue the fight. Instead, she revealed her true purpose—she was gathering powerful rulers to form a new force: the Overlords' alliance. Malqorath was among the few she deemed worthy to stand at her side. She offered him more than alliance—she offered growth, power, and knowledge beyond what he currently possessed.
Ra opposed the idea, unwilling to see his chosen champion align with an unknown force. However, Velmora presented what Malqorath stood to gain if he accepted.
Malqorath chose to join her.
Despite his reluctance, Ra did not interfere. He allowed the decision, recognizing that Malqorath’s path had moved beyond his control.
Before leaving, Velmora gave Malqorath a crystal sphere—a communication artifact that would allow him to reach her when needed.
With that, she vanished.
After Velmora departed, Ra chose not to oppose Malqorath’s decision. Instead, he began preparing him for what lay ahead.
Ra trained Malqorath in the full spectrum of divine desert magic—teaching him not only how to access the power of the gods within him, but how each force functioned individually. Under Ra’s guidance, Malqorath learned control, balance, and the raw nature of divine energy.
When Malqorath questioned why Ra allowed him to join Velmora, Ra revealed his reasoning. He believed that this path would bring greater stability to the desert. Where Ra could teach Malqorath the nature of power, Velmora could push him to master it in ways no god would.
For many years, Malqorath trained, growing far beyond his former limits.
That growth was tested the moment Velmora called for him.
The instant he answered her summon, Malqorath was forced into action—intercepting a deadly strike from Father Crow. He reacted purely on instinct, drawing Zhar’Keth, the God-Chained Fang, and barely managing to block the attack.
The impact nearly killed him.
It was the closest Malqorath had ever come to death since becoming Desert King.
In that moment, he understood the level of threat he had entered. This was not a war of kings—this was a battlefield of monsters far beyond them.
Before the clash could escalate further, Charly intervened and halted the conflict.
As tensions settled, Velmora expressed regret for pulling him into such a confrontation without warning. However, her apology held no weight in Malqorath’s eyes. The encounter had shaken him deeply. For the first time, he had felt fear—not of death, but of being powerless.
His warrior’s pride was fractured.
Despite this, Malqorath honored his agreement. He continued to aid in building the Overlord alliance, though he became distant and reserved. His silence was driven by two forces: anger toward Velmora, and the lingering fear left by Father Crow.
Over time, Charly organized tournaments among the Overlords—battles designed to sharpen their strength. Through these trials, Malqorath slowly rebuilt his confidence. With each fight, he regained pieces of the pride he had lost.
Though he never managed to defeat Father Crow, he ensured that Velmora would never surpass him again.
Eventually, Malqorath reached a point where he believed himself ready. Ready to face Father Crow without hesitation. Ready to erase the fear that haunted him.
But when the time came—
Father Crow was gone.
Vanished without explanation.
Malqorath fought those who took his place, defeating them without difficulty. Yet none of those victories satisfied him. None erased the memory of that single moment.
Without his true opponent, his purpose began to fade.
For the first time since the fall of his village, Malqorath stood on the edge of losing hope.
Years passed, and Malqorath gradually came to believe that Father Crow had vanished for good. The absence of his greatest rival left a void—one that no victory could fill.
That changed when two figures approached his domain:
Orran and Caleb.
They arrived under Father Crow’s orders, sent to reclaim a small region that once belonged to him. At first, Malqorath doubted the claim—but beneath that doubt, something stirred. A long-buried anticipation. A chance to reclaim what he had lost.
Orran immediately recognized it.
He understood that Malqorath did not seek territory—he sought a reason to face Father Crow. Rather than escalate into a true conflict, Orran proposed a solution. Malqorath did not need to prove his strength—only to create the appearance of victory.
Orran chose to stage the outcome.
Avoiding a direct confrontation himself, he forced Caleb into battle against Malqorath, despite Caleb’s reluctance. The fight was real, but its purpose was not. Malqorath defeated Caleb, fulfilling the illusion of resistance.
With the act complete, Orran vanished, leaving Malqorath with a final message of anticipation for what was to come.
One day later—
Father Crow arrived.
After a brief exchange, the battle began. Unlike their first encounter, Malqorath did not hesitate. The fear that once controlled him had been replaced with resolve. The fight was long and relentless, pushing both warriors to their limits.
This time, Malqorath stood his ground.
In the end, he emerged victorious, striking Father Crow down and leaving him unconscious.
Malqorath did not leave.
He remained beside him until Father Crow regained consciousness. When he awoke, Malqorath acknowledged the battle—not with hatred, but with respect. The fight had given him what he had been searching for.
Closure.
With his pride restored, Malqorath allowed Father Crow and his forces to pass freely through his lands, so long as they remained peaceful.
In that moment, the weight he had carried for years finally lifted.
Malqorath chose to let go of his anger—both toward Velmora and the past.
He returned to his throne not as a ruler chasing strength, but as a king who had faced his greatest fear and overcome it.
The Desert King stood once more—unshaken, unbroken, and complete.
